FAQ

ASKED CONSTANTLY
Is this the official Duel merch store?

No. Built by darci, not affiliated with Duel. It's what the store would look like if anyone made it. If Duel wants it to be official, DMs are open.

When can I actually buy one?

Not yet. Everything here is a finished design with real print files behind it, waiting on a production partner. Hit want this made on the ones you'd wear and those go first. No pre-orders and no fake dates.

Why should I trust a merch store that took this long?

The store spent 256 days not existing. This site took one night.

What do you do with my address?

Hand it to the printer and forget it. Your name, email and address go straight to the print partner who makes and ships the garment — this site stores none of it. There's no customer database here, so there's nothing to leak. The order record on our side is just what was ordered and what it cost.

How will payment work?

Crypto only, when it opens. No cards and no account needed. Card processing would mean registering a business and filing paperwork nobody here wants to file.

Can I get my quote on a shirt?

Already happening — the Lousy T-Shirt was sent in by someone in the server and it’s on the wall. Send yours here. If it’s good it goes up.
