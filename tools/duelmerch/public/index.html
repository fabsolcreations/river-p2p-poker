Why this exists

The merch store was scheduled for 2029 by the people who actually work there. It was promised, memed, re-promised, delegated, forgotten, and re-memed. Freefall wanted to reship hoodies out of an EU warehouse. Sean said troll the company. Monarch said "someone go make merch store."

So an outsider did. Designs by the server, receipts by the server, site by darci — no salary, no mandate, no meetings. If the company wants to buy the whole thing — DMs are open.

03 Questions

FAQ

ASKED CONSTANTLY
Is this the official Duel merch store?

No. This is independent — built by darci, unaffiliated, nobody's permission asked. It's just what the merch store looks like when someone who actually wants the merch builds it instead of scheduling it. If Duel wants it to be official, DMs are open.

When can I actually buy one?

Not yet — and we're not going to pretend otherwise. Everything here is a finished design with real print files behind it, waiting on a production partner. Hit want this made on the ones you'd actually wear; those get made first. No pre-orders, no countdown timers, no fake dates. We've had enough of those.

Why should I trust a merch store that took this long?

The store spent 256 days not existing. This site took one night. Different management.

What do you do with my address?

Hand it to the printer and forget it. Your name, email and address go straight to the print partner who makes and ships the garment — this site stores none of it. There's no customer database here, so there's nothing to sell, leak, or hand over. The order record kept on our side is just what was ordered and what it cost.

How will payment work?

Crypto only, when it opens. No cards, no PayPal, no account to make, no ID to upload. Card processing would mean registering a business and filing paperwork nobody here wants to file, and you people were never going to pay for a hoodie any other way.

Can I get my quote on a shirt?

Already happening — the Lousy T-Shirt was a concept someone sent in, and it's in this drop. Send yours here and let natural selection do its thing.
